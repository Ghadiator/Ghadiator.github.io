WORKFLOW VIEW · ORIGINAL MANUAL PROCESS

The original supplier-invoice review workflow

The seven steps this project models.

Manual review · 7 stages
  1. STEP 01

    Request the supplier's documents

    Ask for the invoice, service report and other agreed supporting records.

  2. STEP 02

    Receive and check completeness

    Register the submission, identify missing information and follow up with reminders or clarification requests.

  3. STEP 03

    Check invoice details and calculations

    Reconcile dates, service periods, line items, quantities, rates, taxes and totals for internal consistency.

  4. STEP 04

    Verify against authoritative records

    Compare the invoice with the contract, purchase order, approved rates, spending limits and relevant service evidence.

  5. STEP 05

    Investigate exceptions and corrections

    Review unusual changes or unsupported claims; request explanations or revised documents and check them again.

  6. STEP 06

    Obtain the authorised human decision

    Present the reconciled evidence so an authorised reviewer can approve, reject or request changes.

  7. STEP 07

    Record the outcome and evidence

    Preserve the decision, rationale, document references and review history for later inspection.
