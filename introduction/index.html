The use case · Supplier invoice validation

A routine invoice becomes difficult when the evidence does not agree.

Checking calculations is straightforward; confirming authorised prices, required documents, earlier corrections and unusual claims takes fragmented manual effort. The opportunity is to automate the checks that have definite answers, support exception analysis when evidence is ambiguous, and preserve accountable human decisions.

Repetitive review

Repeated calculation and completeness checks consume reviewer capacity.

Fragmented sources

Contracts, purchase orders, rates and case history are separate sources of authority.

Ambiguous exceptions

Unusual changes need explanation and evidence—not a confident invented answer.

Traceability burden

Every correction, reviewer action and source needs a reconstructible record.
