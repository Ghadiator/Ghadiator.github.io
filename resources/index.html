Concepts behind the engineering figures

  • Chain-of-Verification: answer verification questions independently before a claim is keptFigure: CoVe ladder, CoVe wallDhuliawala et al., “Chain-of-Verification Reduces Hallucination in Large Language Models”, Findings of ACL 2024 (arXiv:2309.11495)
  • Entailment, contradiction and neutral as the three verdicts of a claim against a premiseFigure: CoVe ladderBowman et al., “A large annotated corpus for learning natural language inference”, EMNLP 2015
  • Indirect prompt injection: instructions planted in data that a model later readsFigure: Input sanitisation, red-team probesGreshake et al., “Not what you’ve signed up for: Compromising Real-World LLM-Integrated Applications with Indirect Prompt Injection”, AISec 2023 (arXiv:2302.12173)
  • Confusable characters (homoglyphs) and invisible characters used to hide textFigure: Input sanitisation, pass 2Unicode Technical Standard #39, “Unicode Security Mechanisms”
  • Threat taxonomy for LLM applications, including unbounded consumption (denial of wallet)Figure: Red-team probesOWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2025; MITRE ATLAS
  • Conformance checking: compare a recorded event log with the allowed process modelFigure: Transition catalogueCarmona, van Dongen, Solti and Weidlich, “Conformance Checking: Relating Processes and Models”, Springer 2018
  • Automation bias: people accept an automated result without checking itFigure: Incident window (oversight metrics)Parasuraman and Manzey, “Complacency and Bias in Human Use of Automation”, Human Factors 52(3), 2010; EU AI Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Art. 14(4)(b)
  • Go to a safe state when a dangerous fault is detectedFigure: Incident windowIEC 61508-1:2010, Functional safety of E/E/PE safety-related systems
