Human control · not just a model recommendation

How human oversight is made possible

  1. 01

    Inspect the underlying evidence

    Review the authorized original attachment or verifiably mapped source fields, typed inputs, executed operands and check-time reference values. Unmapped evidence stays labelled.

  2. 02

    Challenge or clarify

    Distinguish hard-rule failures, cross-reference mismatches and AI advice. Open the executed method and request supplier clarification where permitted.

  3. 03

    Make a reasoned decision

    The reviewer selects a permitted action, provides a reason code and written rationale, then explicitly confirms it. A model recommendation is not terminal authority.

  4. 04

    Apply four-eyes control

    A high-risk override or anomaly dismissal is parked. A different reviewer must authorize it before it takes effect.

Explainability contract

What “where” and “how” must mean

Show me where you found it

Original attachment, sheet and cell when verifiable; otherwise the exact recorded typed field and version. Keep submitted facts separate from authoritative reference values.

Show me how you did it

Actual executed check ID, operands, comparison and reference snapshot. Display AI escalation triggers and CoVe separately from any unsupported assertion of internal model causality.

Show me who decided

Reviewer identity, permitted action, reason code, rationale, second approval when applicable, and the ordered audit trace, bound to the correct case version and run.
