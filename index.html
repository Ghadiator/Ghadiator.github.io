Why use AI here?

The workflow does not approve payments: neither a green check nor the AI Judge can. Supplier claims count only when independent records support them. This is a conceptual process, not measured throughput. See how evidence is selected for a case.

Who does what

Governed human–agent collaboration

Operating model: augmented, not autonomous. AI examines and recommends; a named person decides. The support personas answer questions on their own pages and do not run on each case. The Attacker probes copies of the guards, outside the case path.
