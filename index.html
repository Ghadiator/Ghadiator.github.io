SUPPLIER INVOICE REVIEW · GOVERNANCE AS CODE

Checking a supplier invoice, with the decision left to a person.

The job
A supplier sends an invoice every month. Someone must check it against the contract before it is paid.
What this is
A working system that does the checking, proposes what looks wrong, and stops so a named person decides.
Why it is interesting
Every control is visible, testable, and recorded — including the places it is not good enough yet.
