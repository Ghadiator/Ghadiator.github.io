Our eight AI agents

Three independent advisory voices; five evidence-scoped operational assistants. Each has a distinct identity and scope.

Reasoning · 3

Proponent + Opponent → Judge
  • ProponentBuilds an evidence-backed case for the supplier claim
  • OpponentQuestions assumptions, evidence gaps and alternative explanations
  • JudgeReconciles both views into an advisory recommendation

Operational · 5

Bounded tools and evidence
  • AttackerProbes misuse, injection attacks and control weaknesses
  • AnalyticsExamines invoice patterns, exceptions and performance
  • IntegratorConnects contracts, invoices and supporting evidence
  • AuditTraces sources, checks and recorded decision events
  • ReporterPrepares a concise evidence pack for the human reviewer

The use case · Supplier invoice validation

A routine invoice becomes difficult when the evidence does not agree.

Checking calculations is straightforward; confirming authorised prices, required documents, earlier corrections and unusual claims takes fragmented manual effort. The opportunity is to automate checks that have definite answers, support ambiguous cases when needed, and preserve accountable human decisions.

Repetitive review

Repeated calculation and completeness checks consume reviewer capacity.

Fragmented sources

Contracts, purchase orders, rates and case history are separate sources of authority.

Ambiguous exceptions

Unusual changes need explanations and source evidence—not invented answers.

Traceability burden

Every correction, reviewer action and source needs a reconstructible record.

    What changed / source mechanism

    Tier 3 · conditional Proponent / Opponent / CoVe / Judge. Adversarial roles and source verification support the reviewer on exceptional cases only.

    Governance boundary

    Cases resolved by Tier 1 or grounded Tier 2 checks skip the Tier-3 AI committee. Tier 2 may still use an enabled AI selector to choose Python validation primitives. Model claims are proposals, not verified facts or approvals; actual role text is shown only if recorded.

The automation boundary is the argument—not a shorter process.

Tasks still exist even when performed differently. Deterministic validation replaces repeat calculations; enterprise lookups ground claims; AI helps analyse only unresolved exceptions; a named reviewer decides; the audit trail records observed work. Corrections re-enter the same checks. For exact inputs and outputs, inspect a selected recorded or live case.
