Who built this, and why

I make consequential AI governable.

My background is functional safety, where assurance is designed into a system rather than documented after it. The same discipline applies to AI that carries consequences: an obligation becomes a type, a config key, a test or a gate — or it does not exist.

Everything on this page is a working implementation. The traces replay real recorded runs, every evidence path resolves to a file, and each mapping states what it does not establish. I would rather show a smaller claim that holds than a larger one that needs the reader not to look closely.
