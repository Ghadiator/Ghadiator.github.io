AI assurance case

No AI component in this workflow can change what a reviewer decides, or put an unverified figure in front of them as if it were checked.

Deliberately narrow. “The AI is safe” is not a claim this system can discharge, and an assurance case that overreaches discredits the controls it describes. What follows is what the controls can actually carry — and, first, what they cannot.

  • AI is used in exactly one place: Tier 3, after the deterministic checks have run and only for cases they could not settle. A clean invoice never reaches a model.
  • Its output is a PROPOSAL. The terminal states VALIDATION_approved and VALIDATION_rejected may be written only by a reviewer (§11.2.1), and the decision panel refuses without a typed name (ADR-175).
  • No model output reaches the supplier. No model instructs a payment. The workflow stops at a validation decision.
READ THIS FIRST · WHAT THIS CASE DOES NOT ESTABLISH
  1. D1There is no systematic evaluation of model OUTPUT quality. No hallucination rate is measured, no benchmark is run against held-out cases. The argument rests on bounding what a wrong answer can DO, not on how often one occurs.
  2. D2Two findings in the security threat model are open: the admin API accepts unauthenticated destructive calls (T1), and one upload door bypasses every size, type and content guard (T4). Neither is an AI risk, and both would let someone reach the evidence this case rests on.
  3. D3The cross-provider failover tier is configured but not exercisable here: this deployment holds one provider's key, so AI-5's control degrades to the deterministic path under any outage.
  4. D4This is a SELF-assessment. No independent party has reviewed the argument, and no certification is claimed or implied by it.
  5. D5The reference traces and the hazard log describe a supplier-invoice workflow whose engine still carries fields from its pre-migration source domain under renamed labels (ADR-178/180: the words and the stored codes were migrated, the workbook was not). Where a control is argued over a field, read the field, not only its label.

RISK → CONTROL → EVIDENCE → RESIDUAL

The argument, one AI-specific risk at a time

Each control is marked for what it is: 5 are ordinary safety engineering, 2 are exploratory methods doing load-bearing work. Saying so is part of the argument rather than a caveat on it.

AI-1

Fabrication — a model proposes a finding the evidence does not support.

Exploratory method
Why it matters here
A reviewer approves or rejects on a number that was never in the submission, and the audit trail records it as a checked finding.
The control
Chain-of-Verification, twice. Before the Judge, each proposed finding's code, invoice identity and value must match recomputed evidence; after it, the numbers are re-derived and the sentence is REBUILT from them, so the proposal's own wording is discarded rather than trusted.
How we know
  • Look inside the CoVe firewallpipeline/cove_loop.py
  • Minimum-survivor enforcementpipeline/tier3_committee.py
  • Verification teststests/test_cove_loop_adr_091.py

Residual — A claim that is arithmetically true but misleading in its framing survives verification. CoVe checks derivation, not pertinence.

AI-2

Unverifiable consensus — two model roles agree, and both are wrong.

Exploratory method
Why it matters here
Agreement reads as corroboration. Nothing in the record distinguishes two independent judgements from one repeated.
The control
The committee is adversarial by construction: a Proponent argues for acceptance, an Opponent must produce findings. Contested claims are settled by a DETERMINISTIC Python verifier — there is no second model to agree with the first.
How we know
  • Replay a committee case
  • Adversarial evaluationtests/test_adversarial_eval.py
  • Role compliancepipeline/role_compliance.py

Residual — A blind spot in the EVIDENCE is shared by both roles. Adversarial framing finds disagreement about the record; it cannot find what the record omits.

AI-3

Prompt injection — supplier-authored text instructs the model.

Established practice
Why it matters here
A supplier writes its way past review, and the instruction is indistinguishable from the document it arrived in.
The control
Supplier free text is separated from the decision path at the boundary, before anything reads it. Two layers sit there: known injection signatures are redacted deterministically, and — env-gated, off by default — a model reads what the signatures passed and judges intent rather than matching strings, which is what a paraphrased injection requires. The model only ever ADDS a redaction, and only for a span it quoted verbatim. What actually bounds the risk is structural, not textual: the transition catalog permits no machine edge into a human-only verdict, every shared-state model is frozen with extra="forbid", and persona tools are read-only across the board — none of the ten can write a decision, a ticket or a §9 row.
How we know
  • Input sanitiser on the canvas
  • Live red-team campaign
  • Signature coverage and its limitsecurity/ni_sanitiser.py
  • The boundary screen and its four rulessecurity/injection_classifier.py
  • Toolset budget and groupsdashboard_agents/toolsets.py

Residual — Both layers are defence in depth, and neither makes the input trustworthy. Signature matching provably ends: four live red-team campaigns each closed the payloads the last one found, and each time the attacker returned with the same intent in wording no pattern held — by the fourth round with no covered signature in the payload at all (ADR-181). The LLM screen catches those (5 of 5 measured, 8 of 8 legitimate clauses untouched), but it is a model reading attacker-authored text, so it is itself a target: delimiting the text is mitigation, not proof, and its judgement is not reproducible the way a regex is — which is why a separate marker keeps the two apart in the audit chain, and why it may only add redactions, never remove one (ADR-182). Injection carried by a NUMBER rather than prose is addressed by neither layer: a figure chosen to steer a threshold reads as ordinary data.

AI-4

Non-determinism — the same case does not produce the same assessment twice.

Established practice
Why it matters here
An audit cannot reproduce the finding it is auditing, and severity drifts between runs of the same evidence.
The control
The Judge emits tag NAMES from a closed vocabulary; their risk weights are fixed in versioned code and attached deterministically after verification (ADR-142), so severity never varies with phrasing or temperature.
How we know
  • The anomaly-tag catalog and its weights
  • Band coverage testticketing/tests/test_risk_weight_bands.py
  • Catalog sourcemodels/semantic_grounding.py

Residual — The structured finding is reproducible; the prose rationale beside it is not, and is advisory only.

AI-5

Silent degradation — the model stops answering and the system keeps reporting.

Established practice
Why it matters here
A deterministic fallback result is presented with the authority of an AI assessment, or a surface claims providers are healthy while every call fails.
The control
Tiered failover with a deterministic last resort that is LABELLED as such, and a reachability probe that makes one real call per configured provider rather than inferring health from a key being present.
How we know
  • Provider probe (the chip in the top bar)ticketing/api/routes_meta.py
  • Missing-fallback-key behaviourtests/test_fallback_missing_key.py
  • Live-gate follows the configured providertests/test_live_gate_follows_configured_provider.py

Residual — With ONE provider configured the cross-provider tier is nominal: an outage drops every persona to its deterministic path. That is this deployment's current state, not a hypothetical.

AI-6

Authority creep — the proposal becomes the decision in practice.

Established practice
Why it matters here
The human gate is satisfied formally while the outcome is set by the model, and the audit trail cannot tell the two apart.
The control
Only a reviewer may write a terminal state (§11.2.1). The §9 stream is append-only with a CLOSED transition catalog, so a state the catalog does not contain cannot be appended at all, by any actor.
How we know
  • The §9 trail, by actor
  • Transition conformancetelemetry/conformance.py
  • Open-posture attributionticketing/tests/test_open_workbench_posture.py

Residual — A reviewer who approves without reading satisfies every control here. Attribution is recorded; attention is not measurable from the record.

AI-7

Context bleed — one case's material reaches another case's assessment.

Established practice
Why it matters here
A finding is justified by evidence belonging to a different supplier, and the citation looks sound.
The control
One case per LLM context, enforced rather than advised, with a generic-lesson lint so learning written back carries no case-specific material (ADR-141).
How we know
  • Lessons ledger
  • Case-data containmentADR-141

Residual — The guard bounds what enters a context. It cannot bound what a model retained from its own training.
