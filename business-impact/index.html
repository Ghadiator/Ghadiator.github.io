Business case · Supplier invoice validation

Business value & KPI evidence

How does governed agentic AI change invoice review? The value comes from putting definite calculations into code, testing external evidence independently, and spending model reasoning on exceptions—while retaining human accountability.

This is a business-impact hypothesis supported by workflow evidence, not a claim of measured production ROI. Time savings, accuracy improvements and cost reductions require matched before/after data that the public supplier fixtures do not provide.

01 · Business profile

Where time and money are at risk

An organisation receives supplier invoices and supporting workbooks. Reviewers must confirm quantities and totals, agreed rates, purchase-order scope, document completeness and the legitimacy of corrected versions. A correct calculation alone does not show that a charge was authorised.

Routine effort

Recurring arithmetic and format checks

Evidence retrieval

Contracts, rate cards and purchase orders

Exception resolution

Unclear deviations and contested claims

Decision accountability

Named reviewer and reconstructible rationale

02 · Improvement mechanism

What changes when agentic reasoning is governed

01

Tier 1 · exact checks

Arithmetic, schema and completeness are resolved with deterministic code.

02

Tier 2 · evidence cross-check

Approved contracts, orders and rates ground Python primitives. Optional AI chooses relevant checks.

03

Tier 3 · disputed exceptions

Proponent, Opponent and Judge debate unresolved claims; CoVe tests factual support.

04

Human authorisation

The reviewer decides; an AI recommendation is never an invoice approval.

The AI-specific contribution is selective tool choice and bounded exception reasoning. The deterministic checks, source-control boundaries and human gate do most of the reliability work; they should not be mislabeled as a productivity result generated by an LLM.

03 · Measurable KPIs

Six KPIs, with their improvement path and evidence status

Measured behavior ≠ measured business impact

Reviewer touch time

Before · Manual pressure

Manually check invoice lines, supporting documents and contractual exceptions.

After · Designed improvement

Tier 1 calculates exact checks; Tier 2 retrieves independent terms; only unresolved cases need committee reasoning.

How we would verify the improvement

Sum of reviewer-active minutes ÷ eligible invoices, comparing matched before/after cohorts.

Evidence status: Not measured for these supplier examples. No unsourced percentage improvement is asserted.

04 · API and backend boundary

What the API enables—and what the public demo does not

Authenticated operations

Protected case access, reviewer identity, persistence and authorised decisions require the backend—not a static site.

Independent source contracts

Enterprise records, contract clauses, purchase orders and rates must be supplied by authorised integrations. A sample document is not a live retrieval.

Operational measurement

Real API events can supply processing timestamps, reviewer touch measurements, model tokens and costs. Without those, there is no proven before/after ROI.

GitHub Pages is a public read-only reference with synthetic evidence and a local workbook preflight. The protected application exposes backend capabilities only within its configured authentication and service boundary. Discussion with a persona does not invoke the Tier-3 adjudication pipeline or authorise a supplier payment.
