Classification before compliance

“High-risk” is a legal conclusion, not a design style.

The intended purpose here is reviewing supplier billing submissions against approved contracts and rate cards. Classification follows the intended purpose, not the architecture — so this showcase asserts no Annex III high-risk classification, and makes no claim of conformity assessment or certification.

Actor analysis
