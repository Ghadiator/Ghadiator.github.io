Incidents & learning

Two channels sit alongside the workflow: incidents trigger protective responses, while accumulated evidence can become a proposed lesson. Human governance determines what changes. A lesson never activates itself as a new policy.

How each channel works

Each stage below says what mechanism carries it and, where it writes a record, opens the ledger holding that record.

Evidence → learning → human governance

Lessons-learned channel

  1. 01

    Recorded evidence

    ProcessEvent + reviewer outcome

    The §9 event history and the reviewer's terminal decision are the evidence a lesson can rest on.

    Open the §9 event trail
  2. 02

    Automatic extraction

    Deterministic lesson aggregator

    Runs synchronously after a confirmed validation batch and proposes deduplicated, evidence-linked candidates for any slice with at least five supporting cases. It reads the §9 stream from the host-local file, so it does nothing while the stream is on the shared SQL backend.

  3. 03

    Structured record

    Lessons-learned ledger

    Each lesson persists with its source, topic, evidence IDs, support count, proposed action and status.

  4. 04

    Human decision

    Acknowledge · Integrate · Reject

    A reviewer decides, and can link an integrated lesson to an ADR. Marking a lesson never changes runtime behaviour.

Optional, off unless configured

LLM lesson synthesis

A separate pass can propose open lessons from bounded §9 event windows. It runs only where configuration and provider credentials permit.

Ruleset proposal + shadow comparison

An integrated lesson carrying a valid knob directive can generate a challenger ruleset. Default OFF; shadow results are recorded, never activated.

Human authority boundary: no learned lesson and no shadow challenger becomes the active validation policy on its own.

Detection → record → protective gate

Incident monitoring channel

  1. 01

    Monitor

    Fairness and human-oversight signals

    Detection functions can raise alignment incidents, subject to their scan and data conditions.

  2. 02

    Record

    IncidentEvent + incident store

    Structured kind, severity, description, linked trace IDs and mitigation status.

  3. 03

    Fail-safe check

    A recent incident blocks auto-approval

    Where straight-through processing is enabled, a HIGH or CRITICAL incident inside 60 minutes prevents automatic approval — and a missing shared incident store fails closed.

The gate only ever withholds automatic approval. It cannot approve, and an unreachable incident store fails closed rather than open.

Scope: these channels are implemented in this deployment, and whether they fire depends on the triggers and configuration described above — a quiet channel means its conditions were not met, not that it is absent. One condition is worth stating plainly: the lesson aggregator reads the host-local §9 file, so on a multi-host deployment (VALIDATION_PROCESS_EVENTS_BACKEND=sql) it proposes nothing at all. The incident channel has no such limit — it reads through the registered store and fails closed. Neither channel writes to the §9 stream or alters a component on the architecture canvas: they record, and a reviewer decides.
