Why does this matter?

Typed contracts stop malformed data at the boundary.

Example: an invoice says 3 units × €120 = €360. Pydantic validates the declared fields and types. Tier 1 then checks the arithmetic; Tier 2 checks whether €120 is an approved rate. A structured LLM claim is still just a claim until verified.

Pydantic does

Types · fields · declared constraints · structured handoff

It does not

Prove facts · approve rates · verify the LLM · make a human decision
