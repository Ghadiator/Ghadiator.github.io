AI roles & controls

Each role answers over a bounded slice of the system, and the boundary is the point: none of them can take a decision. In the reviewer implementation, answers draw on the process-event chain, case evidence and project documents. This public view explains the roles; it does not run persona chats.

Reasoning personas

Three bounded model roles contribute advisory reasoning. Chain-of-Verification (CoVe) is an evidence control between roles, not a fourth AI persona.

Proponent

Argues the strongest evidence-based case supporting acceptance.

Advisory only; cannot set final disposition.

Inspect in a recorded case →

Opponent

Challenges assumptions and presents potential deviations and risks.

Candidate claims must be corroborated independently.

Inspect in a recorded case →

Judge

Synthesizes argument and surviving verified findings for human review.

Cannot approve, reject, pay or close a case.

Operational personas

System-scoped assistants provide explanations, retrieval and analysis without owning the human decision. The case-bound Human Reviewer belongs to the relevant case's decision context.

  • Integrator

    Code, external interfaces and governance questions

    Answers only from the project's own documents — ADRs, the system card, integration contracts and the RAI principles. It cannot probe the running system.

    Related evidence on Integration, beside the work it talks about.

  • Audit

    Evidence and state transitions

    Read-only over the §9 chain. It reconstructs why the system did what it did and names the rule that governs it. It never decides (ADR-078).

    Related evidence on Process audit, beside the work it talks about.

  • Reports

    Case summaries on demand

    Read-only across the §9 stream, the lessons ledger and the system card. It writes prose about records; it cannot change one.

    Related evidence on Cases, beside the work it talks about.

  • Analytics

    Cost, ROI and pool evidence

    Reports the benchmark cohorts and the pool rollup as they stand, including when a cohort is empty — an absent number is reported as absent, not smoothed.

    Related evidence on Dashboard, beside the work it talks about.

  • Red team

    Attack surface and guards

    Probes run against hermetic copies of the defence code. No adversarial traffic ever reaches the running system.

    Related evidence on Red team, beside the work it talks about.
