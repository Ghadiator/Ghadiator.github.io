PERSONAS · SCOPE · CONTROLS

AI roles & controls

Each role answers over a bounded slice of the system, and the boundary is the point: advisory AI does not authorise invoices. There are exactly three reasoning personas and five operational assistants. The protected workbench provides separate read-only reasoning discussions; these never invoke the live Tier-3 committee, load an unauthorised case or substitute for the human gate. Public visitors can inspect the role explanations and starter questions.

Reasoning personas

Three bounded model roles contribute advisory reasoning. Chain-of-Verification (CoVe) is an evidence control between roles, not a fourth AI persona.

Proponent

Argues the strongest evidence-based case supporting acceptance.

Advisory only; cannot set final disposition.

Inspect in a recorded case →

Opponent

Challenges assumptions and presents potential deviations and risks.

Candidate claims must be corroborated independently.

Inspect in a recorded case →

Judge

Synthesizes argument and surviving verified findings for human review.

Cannot approve, reject, pay or close a case.

Inspect in a recorded case →

Discuss with Proponent · Opponent · Judge

Ask how each role would evaluate a hypothetical invoice exception, what evidence it would require, or which argument it would challenge. The protected conversation is an explanatory session—not a case-specific verdict, CoVe result, payment authorisation or replay of the workflow's actual committee.

CoVe and the authority boundary

Pre-Judge CoVe tests claims against invoice and contract evidence; downstream verification and the human review gate provide additional separation. A verified model claim remains an advisory finding—not a final decision.

Operational personas

System-scoped assistants provide explanations, retrieval and analysis without owning the human decision. The case-bound Human Reviewer belongs to the relevant case's decision context.

  • Integrator

    Code, external interfaces and governance questions

    Answers only from the project's own documents — ADRs, the system card, integration contracts and the RAI principles. It cannot probe the running system.

    Starter question: What are the integration seams?

    Evidence and state transitions

    Case summaries on demand

    Read-only across the §9 stream, the lessons ledger and the system card. It writes prose about records; it cannot change one.
